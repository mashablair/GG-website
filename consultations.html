Good Questions

Before you book

Is this coaching or therapy?

Coaching. Mentorship. I'm not licensed to do therapy, and I won't pretend to be. If it becomes clear you'd be better served by a therapist, I'll say so — respectfully, and because I think you deserve the right room.

Do you promise I'll meet someone?

No, and neither should anyone else. What I promise is that you'll leave with more clarity than you came in with. That's what changes what happens next.

What if I don't have a specific situation — I'm just… tired?

That's a situation. Book the call. We'll figure out where the tired is coming from together.

Can I book more than one?

Yes. Some women use it as a monthly reset. I don't push it — you'll know if you want another one.
