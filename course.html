Good to Know

Questions, answered

I'm not divorced. Is this for me?

Yes — if you've been quiet for a while, whatever the reason got you there. Long single stretches, dead relationships, bad or confusing dating experiences that closed the door. The Method starts from the same place.

Is this just about apps?

No. Apps are one channel. The Method is about the woman first. That's what makes it work in person, at events, and yes — on the apps too.

How long does it take?

Move at your own pace — you have a full year of access. Most women complete all the modules and do the work in 1–2 weeks. Once you understand my philosophy, you'll feel empowered to keep going on your own, with confidence and clarity.

What if I'm scared?

Good. That means you're finally paying attention to what you want. Everything you want in life is just outside your comfort zone.
