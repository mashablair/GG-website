The Course

The Intentional Dating Method

For women 35+ who want to date with intention, not desperation.

Save My Spot 🐆

This course is for you if…

  • You left a long relationship and the idea of dating feels overwhelming
  • You've been alone for a while and you're ready — but scared
  • You've tried dating apps and it felt random, chaotic, or demoralizing
  • You're tired of advice from married friends who haven't dated in 15 years
  • You want to feel confident and feminine on dates, not like you're auditioning

What you'll learn

01

Inner Readiness First

Before the apps, the real work: self-worth, calming your nervous system, healing what needs healing. You show up whole, not desperate — because everything downstream depends on this.

02

Feminine Energy on Dates

How to stop auditioning and start receiving. What feminine energy actually means in practice — on a first date, in conversation, in how you let yourself be treated. You observe. You enjoy. That's the whole secret.

03

Your Personal Dating System

The engineer's approach: choosing your one platform, building an honest profile, the photos that actually work, safety rules, clear standards, handling rejection, and knowing when to commit. No chaos. Just intentional reps.

Who teaches this

Maria Blair

I'm not a guru. I'm a woman who did it — 60+ intentional dates after leaving a 17-year marriage, every one of them a lesson I wrote down.

Twenty years as a software engineer taught me to build systems, so I built one for dating. My health coaching background taught me the inner work comes first. And being a single mom taught me I don't have time for chaos — and neither do you.
